NGC 115 is a barred spiral galaxy located in the southern constellation of Sculptor. It was discovered by the British astronomer John Herschel on September 25, 1834. The galaxy is approximately 85 million light-years from the Sun, and is about 50,000 light-years in diameter, nearly half the size of our home galaxy, the Milky Way. NGC 115 belongs to the galaxy group known as NGC 134 Group or LGG 7. It's members are NGC 131, IC 1554, NGC 148, IC 1555, NGC 134 and ESO 410-18.
