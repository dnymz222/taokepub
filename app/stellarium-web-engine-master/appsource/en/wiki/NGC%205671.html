NGC 5671 is a large barred spiral galaxy in the constellation of Ursa Minor. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 9,058±43 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 435.7 ± 30.6 Mly (133.59 ± 9.39 Mpc). However, five non-redshift measurements give a closer mean distance of 353.75 ± 10.07 Mly (108.460 ± 3.086 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 6 May 1791. According to Abraham Mahtessian, NGC 5671 and UGC 9295 form a pair of galaxies.

SN 2008be (Type IIn, mag. 18.5) was discovered by the Lick Observatory Supernova Search (LOSS) on 12 March 2008. SN 2020dya (Type II, mag. 19.11) was discovered by the Zwicky Transient Facility on 4 March 2020.
