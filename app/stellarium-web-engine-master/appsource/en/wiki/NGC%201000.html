NGC 1000 is an elliptical galaxy located in the northern constellation of Andromeda. It was discovered on December 9, 1871 by French astronomer Édouard Jean-Marie Stephan. It is the 1,000th object included in the New General Catalogue. NGC 1000 has an apparent visual magnitude of 15.6 and an angular size of 0.8′ × 0.7′. It is located at a distance of 195.1 million light-years (59.82 Mpc) from the Milky Way.
