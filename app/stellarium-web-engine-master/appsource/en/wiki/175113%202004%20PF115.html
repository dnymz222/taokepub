(175113) 2004 PF115 is a trans-Neptunian object (TNO). It was discovered in 2006 by M. Brown, C. Trujillo, and D. Rabinowitz. The object is classified as a possible plutino. The Hubble Space Telescope photographed 2004 PF115 on 8 September 2026, during a search for moons around large trans-Neptunian objects.

Physical characteristics

The diameter of 2004 PF115 was measured by the Herschel Space Telescope to be 406.3+97.6−75.3 km.
