NGC 365 is a barred spiral galaxy in the constellation Sculptor. It was discovered on November 25, 1834, by John Herschel. It was described by Dreyer as "faint, small, round, gradually a little brighter middle." One supernova has been observed in NGC 365: SN 1970N (type unknown, mag. 18.8) was discovered by Steven van Agt on 4 August 1970.
