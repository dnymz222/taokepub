(673087) 2015 AJ281 is a magnitude-5.0 trans-Neptunian object that was discovered by La Silla Observatory on 25 March 2011 and given the provisional designation 2011 FW62. Its orbital elements were very uncertain and it was lost. It was recovered on 6 January 2015 as 2015 AJ281. 2015 AJ281 has been identified as a member of the Haumea family in a dynamical study led by Proudfoot and Ragozzine in 2019.
