NGC 1184 is a lenticular galaxy in the constellation of Cepheus. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 2,269±20 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 109.2 ± 7.8 Mly (33.47 ± 2.38 Mpc). It was discovered by British astronomer William Herschel on 16 September 1787. NGC 1184 has a possible active galactic nucleus, i.e. it has a compact region at the center of a galaxy that emits a significant amount of energy across the electromagnetic spectrum, with characteristics indicating that this luminosity is not produced by the stars.

Galaxy group

NGC 1184 is member of a small galaxy group known as nest 101283. The other two galaxies in the group are UGC 2519 and UGC 2620.
