NGC 6676 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation of Draco. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 6707 ± 12 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 98.92 ± 6.93 Mpc (~323 million light-years). It was discovered by American astronomer Lewis Swift on 30 May 1886. One supernova has been observed in NGC 6676: SN 2023txu (Type Ia, mag 18.96) was discovered by ATLAS on 3 October 2023.
