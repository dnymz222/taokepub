NGC 7836 is an irregular spiral galaxy located about 260 million light-years away in the constellation Andromeda. It is part of the Perseus–Pisces Supercluster. This galaxy was discovered by American astronomer Lewis Swift on September 20, 1885. It has an apparent visual magnitude of 14.4 and spans an angular size of 0.9′ × 0.5′. NGC 7836 is a member of the NGC 7831 Group (also known as LGG 1), which contains at least 18 galaxies.
