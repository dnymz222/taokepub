NGC 3961 is a barred spiral galaxy in the constellation of Draco. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 6,807±7 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 327.5 ± 22.9 Mly (100.41 ± 7.03 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 7 April 1793.

Pair of galaxies

NGC 3961 and UGC 6844 form a pair of galaxies.
