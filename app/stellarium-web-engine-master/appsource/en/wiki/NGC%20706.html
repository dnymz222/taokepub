NGC 706 is a spiral galaxy located in the Pisces constellation about 230 million light years from the Milky Way. It was discovered by German–British astronomer William Herschel in 1786.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 706:

SN 2001ed (Type Ia, mag. 14.9) was discovered by Marco Migliardi on 21 August 2001.
