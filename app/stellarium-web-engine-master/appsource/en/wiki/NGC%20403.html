NGC 403 is a lenticular galaxy located in the constellation of Pisces. It was discovered on August 29, 1862, by Heinrich d'Arrest. NGC 403 is considered to be part of a galaxy group known as LGG 18 or NGC 452 Group. It has at least 17 members, which are NGC 383, NGC 385, NGC 399, NGC 382, NGC 420, NGC 443, NGC 444, NGC 392, NGC 452, NGC 374, NGC 388, NGC 410, NGC 414, IC 1654, IC 1618 and IC 1652.
