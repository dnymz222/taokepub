(26375) 1999 DE9 is a trans-Neptunian object. Light-curve-amplitude analysis shows only small deviations, suggesting 1999 DE9 is a spheroid with small albedo spots. Measurements by the Spitzer Space Telescope estimate that it is 461 ± 45 km in diameter. It was discovered in 1999 by Chad Trujillo and Jane X. Luu. 1999 DE9's orbit is in 2:5 resonance with Neptune's. Spectral analysis has shown traces of ice.
