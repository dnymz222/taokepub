NGC 484 is an elliptical galaxy in the constellation Tucana. It is located approximately 218 million light-years from Earth and was discovered on October 28, 1834 by astronomer John Herschel. NGC 484 is part of a galaxy group known as NGC 434 Group or LGG 19. It has at least 10 members, which are NGC 434, NGC 440, NGC 466, NGC 434A, IC 1649, ESO 113-27, ESO 151-27, ESO 114-1 and ESO 113-35.
