NGC 3197 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation of Draco. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 8,146±4 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 391.9 ± 27.4 Mly (120.15 ± 8.41 Mpc). However, three non-redshift measurements give a closer mean distance of 348.99 ± 4.98 Mly (107.000 ± 1.528 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 2 April 1801.

SN 2005kx (Type II, mag. 17.3) was discovered by Tim Puckett and J. Tigner on 26 November 2005. SN 2022xqp (Type Ia, mag. 19.7221) was discovered by the Zwicky Transient Facility on 14 October 2022.
