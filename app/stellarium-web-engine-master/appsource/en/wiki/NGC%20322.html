NGC 322 is a lenticular galaxy located approximately 318 million light-years from the Solar System in the constellation Phoenix. It was discovered on September 5, 1834 by John Herschel. It was described by Dreyer as "very faint, very small, round, a little brighter middle, 3 stars to west." It apparently seems to be interacting with PGC 95427, another galaxy. From legacy survey images a faint ring or tidal feature can be seen around NGC 322.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 322:

SN 2018bwv (Type Ia, mag. 16.2), was discovered by ASAS-SN on 23 May 2018.
