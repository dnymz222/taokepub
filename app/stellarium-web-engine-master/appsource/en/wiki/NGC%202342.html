NGC 2342 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation of Gemini. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 5445 ± 11 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 80.31 ± 5.62 Mpc (~262 million light-years). It was discovered by German astronomer Albert Marth on 10 November 1864. NGC 2342 is a luminous infrared galaxy (LIRG). Together with NGC 2341, they both form a gravitationally bound galaxy pair listed as HOLM 86.
