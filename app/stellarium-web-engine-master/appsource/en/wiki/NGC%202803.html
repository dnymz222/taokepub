NGC 2803, also known as PCG 26181, is an elliptical or lenticular galaxy in the zodiac constellation Cancer. It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel 21 March 1784. It is interacting with NGC 2802.

Supernova

Two supernovae have been observed in NGC 2803:

SN 2017ilf (Type Ia, mag. 18) was discovered by ASAS-SN on 22 November 2017. SN 2026afcx (Type Ia-91bg-like, mag. 17.73) was discovered by Ziyang Mai, Hongzhi Liang, and Zhenghan Xiong on 10 October 2026.
