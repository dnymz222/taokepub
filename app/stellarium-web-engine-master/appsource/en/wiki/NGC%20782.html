NGC 782 is a barred spiral galaxy located in the constellation Eridanus about 160 million light-years from the Milky Way. It was discovered by British astronomer John Herschel in 1834.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 782. SN 2011eb (Type Ia, mag. 15.2) was discovered by Berto Monard on July 9, 2011. It was positioned 29″ west and 34″ south of the galactic core.
