NGC 7634 is a barred lenticular galaxy in the constellation of Pegasus. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 2,844±32 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 136.8 ± 9.8 Mly (41.94 ± 2.99 Mpc). However, six non-redshift measurements give a farther mean distance of 175.47 ± 5.89 Mly (53.800 ± 1.805 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 26 September 1785.

NGC 7619 group

NGC 7634 is a member of the NGC 7619 group (also known as LGG 473). This galaxy group contains at least 25 members, including NGC 7518, NGC 7557, NGC 7562, NGC 7604, NGC 7608, NGC 7610, NGC 7611, NGC 7612, NGC 7619, NGC 7623, NGC 7626, NGC 7631, NGC 7648, IC 1474, and eight others.

Supernova

SN 1972J (Type I, mag. 15) was discovered by Luisa Pigatto on 13 August 1972.
