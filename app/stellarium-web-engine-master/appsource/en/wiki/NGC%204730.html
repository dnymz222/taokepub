NGC 4730 is a lenticular galaxy located about 160 million light-years away in the constellation Centaurus. NGC 4730 was discovered by astronomer John Herschel on June 8, 1834. NGC 4730 is a member of the Centaurus Cluster. NGC 4730 is considered to be part of a galaxy group known as NGC 4751 Group or LGG 309. It has at least NGC 4751, NGC 4730, ESO 322-101, ESO 323-33, PGC 43383, PGC 43402, PGC 43553, PGC 43722.
