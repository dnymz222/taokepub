NGC 6560 is a spiral galaxy located in the constellation Hercules. It was discovered by Lewis A. Swift on 22 October 1886.

Supernovae

Three supernovae have been observed in NGC 6560:

SN 2021brb (Type II, mag. 18.8747) was discovered by Automatic Learning for the Rapid Classification of Events (ALeRCE) on 3 February 2021. SN 2021qqn (Type Ia, mag. 18.5107) was discovered by ALeRCE on 21 June 2021. SN 2022oyp (Type II, mag. 19.6437) was discovered by ALeRCE on 17 July 2022.
