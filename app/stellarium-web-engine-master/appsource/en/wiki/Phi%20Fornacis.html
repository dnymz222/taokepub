The distance to this object is approximately 154 light-years based on parallax, and it is drifting further away with a radial velocity of +19 km/s. It has 2.1 times the mass of the Sun and 1.7 times the Sun's radius. The star is radiating 17 times the luminosity of the Sun from its photosphere at an effective temperature of 9,449 km/s.
