NGC 5442 is a barred spiral galaxy in the constellation of Virgo. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 6,130±26 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 294.9 ± 20.7 Mly (90.41 ± 6.34 Mpc). It was discovered by German astronomer Albert Marth on 11 January 1865.

Supernovae

Three supernovae have been observed in NGC 5442:

SN 2001U (Type Ia, mag. 17.7) was discovered by LOTOSS (Lick Observatory and Tenagra Observatory Supernova Searches) on 3 February 2001. SN 2011bz (Type Ia, mag. 17.4) was discovered by the Catalina Real-time Transient Survey on 24 April 2011. SN 2024jrx (Type II, mag. 19.3244) was discovered by Automatic Learning for the Rapid Classification of Events (ALeRCE) on 27 May 2024.
