164P/Christensen, also known as Comet Christensen 2, is a Jupiter-family comet with a 6.97-year orbit around the Sun. It is one of several comets discovered by American astronomer, Eric J. Christensen.

Observational history

The comet was discovered by Eric J. Christensen in late 2004 from images in the Catalina Sky Survey. It was the second comet he discovered after 210P/Christensen. At the time of discovery the comet had an apparent magnitude of 16.5 in the constellation Leo.
