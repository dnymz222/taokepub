NGC 4076 is a spiral galaxy located about 314 million light-years away in the constellation Coma Berenices. The galaxy was discovered by astronomer William Herschel on April 27, 1785 and is a member of the NGC 4065 Group. NGC 4076 is classified as a LINER galaxy.

Supernovae

NGC 4076 has been host to two supernovae.

SN 2007M (Type Ia, mag. 19.2) was discovered by the Lick Observatory Supernova Search (LOSS) on January 14, 2007. It was also seen in a precovery image taken on December 24, 2006. SN 2011bc (Type Ia, mag. 17.3) was discovered by Ron Arbour and N. Kojima on April 1, 2011.
