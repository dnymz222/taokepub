NGC 662 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation of Andromeda. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 5,397 ± 18 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 79.6 ± 5.6 Mpc (~260 million light-years). It was discovered by French astronomer Édouard Stephan on 22 November 1884. NGC 662 features a broad HI line and is classified a field galaxy, meaning it does not belong to the galaxy group nor a cluster and is therefore gravitationally isolated.

Supernovae

Three supernovae have been observed in NGC 662:

SN 2001dn (Type Ia, mag. 15.5) was discovered by Tom Boles on 14 August 2001. SN 2022ojo (Type II-P, mag. 16.938) was discovered by ATLAS on 5 July 2022. SN 2024pcw (Type II, mag. 18.724) was discovered by ATLAS on 8 July 2024.
