NGC 182 is a spiral galaxy with a ring structure, located in the constellation Pisces. Astronomer William Herschel discovered it on December 25, 1790. A Type IIb supernova, designated SN 2004ex, was discovered in this galaxy in 2004. NGC 182 is located in a galaxy group known as NGC 198 Group or LGG 9, together with NGC 198, NGC 194 and NGC 200.
