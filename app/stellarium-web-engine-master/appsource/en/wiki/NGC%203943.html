NGC 3943 is a barred lenticular galaxy, located 304 million light-years away in the Leo constellation. It was discovered on April 27, 1865, by astronomer Heinrich d'Arrest. NGC 3943 is a LINER galaxy, and is a member of the NGC 3937 Group. NGC 3943 has a supermassive black hole with an estimated mass of 2.4 × 108 M☉.
