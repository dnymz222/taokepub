NGC 1993 (also known as ESO 554-14) is a lenticular galaxy located in the Lepus constellation. It was discovered by John Herschel on February 6, 1835. It is about 143 million light years from the Milky Way. Its apparent magnitude is 13.39 and its size is 1.5 arc minutes. One supernova has been observed in NGC 1993: SN 2024sag (type Ia-91bg-like, mag. 18.7).
