NGC 4217 is an edge-on spiral galaxy which lies approximately 60 million light-years (18 million parsecs) away in the constellation of Canes Venatici. It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 10 April 1788. It is a possible companion galaxy to Messier 106 (NGC 4258).

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 4217:

SN 2022myz (Type I, mag. 19.064) was discovered by the Zwicky Transient Facility on 19 June 2022.
