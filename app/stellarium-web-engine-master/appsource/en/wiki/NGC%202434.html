NGC 2434 is an elliptical galaxy located in the Volans Constellation. It was discovered by John Herschel in 1864. He described it as faint, small, round and pretty much brighter in the middle. There are three foreground stars in the same field of view, 11 arcminutes north of NGC 2434. It is part of a galaxy group known as LGG 147, which consists of at least of five members. The members of the group are NGC 2397, NGC 2442, NGC 2443, NGC 2434 and PGC 20690. NGC 2434 has a diameter of 31.29 kiloparsecs. Roughly half its mass within an effective radius is dark matter.
