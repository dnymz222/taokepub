NGC 834 is a spiral galaxy located in the Andromeda constellation. It is estimated to be 160 million light-years away from the Milky Way galaxy and has a diameter of about 65,000 light-years. The object was discovered on September 21, 1786 by the astronomer William Herschel. NGC 834 is a member of a galaxy group the known as LGG 51, which contains six galaxies, such as NGC 841, UGC 1721, UGC 1771, UGC 1650 and UGC 1673.
