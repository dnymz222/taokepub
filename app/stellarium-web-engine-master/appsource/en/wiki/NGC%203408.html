NGC 3408 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation of Ursa Major. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 9,621±11 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 462.8 ± 32.4 Mly (141.91 ± 9.94 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 8 April 1793. NGC 3408 is a radio galaxy, i.e. it has giant regions of radio emission extending well beyond its visible structure.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 3408:

SN 2018ka (Type Ia, mag. 19.4) was discovered by R. Gagliano, R. Post, E. Weinberg, Jack B. Newton, and Tim Puckett on 17 January 2018.
