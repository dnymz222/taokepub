Psi Cygni is a triple star system in the constellation of Cygnus. Its name is a Bayer designation that is Latinized from ψ Cygni and is abbreviated Psi Cyg or ψ Cyg. With a combined apparent visual magnitude of 4.92, it is visible to the naked eye as a point of light. Based upon an annual parallax shift of 11.55 mas, Psi Cygni is located at a distance of around 282 light years from the Sun. As of 2002, the inner pair, components Aa and Ab, had an angular separation of 0.10 arc seconds along a position angle of 77.6°. Their combined visual magnitude is 5.05. Relative to this pair, the third member of the system, magnitude 7.61 component B, had an angular separation of 2.87 arc seconds along a position angle of 175.6° as of 2010. The brighter member of the system, presumably component Aa, displays the spectrum of an A-type main sequence star with a stellar classification of A4 Vn, where the 'n' notation indicates "nebulous" absorption lines due to rapid rotation. It is spinning rapidly with a projected rotational velocity of 207 km/s. The component is radiating 62 times the solar luminosity from its outer atmosphere at an effective temperature of 7,971 K. Component B has a spectral class of F4 V, matching an F-type main-sequence star. It is spinning with a high projected rotational velocity of 120 km/s.
