The Bayer designation Iota Librae (ι Lib / ι Librae) is shared by two star systems, in the constellation Libra:

ι1 Librae (24 Librae), also known simply as ι Librae (iota Librae) ι2 Librae (25 Librae) The pair have an angular separation of just 17′. They can be viewed as a double star with a pair of binoculars or a small telescope.
