NGC 1643 is a peculiar barred spiral galaxy in the constellation of Eridanus. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 4,834±4 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 232.5 ± 16.3 Mly (71.30 ± 4.99 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 28 November 1786.

Supernovae

Two supernovae have been observed in NGC 1643:

SN 1995G (Type IIn, mag. 15.5) was discovered by Robert Evans, J. Shobbrook, and S. Beaman, on 23 February 1995. SN 1999et (Type II, mag. 17.6) was discovered by E. Cappellaro on 4 November 1999.
