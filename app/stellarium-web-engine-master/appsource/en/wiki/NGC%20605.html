NGC 605 is a lenticular galaxy in the constellation Andromeda, which is about 234 million light-years from the Milky Way. It was discovered on October 21, 1881 by the French astronomer Édouard Jean-Marie Stephan. SIMBAD lists NGC 605 as an active galaxy nucleus candidate. One supernova has been observed in NGC 605: SN 2024nnu (type Ia, mag. 15.6).
