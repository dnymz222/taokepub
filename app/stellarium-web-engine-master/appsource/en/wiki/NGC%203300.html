NGC 3300 is a lenticular galaxy in the constellation Leo. It was discovered by the astronomer William Herschel on 19 March 1784. NGC 3300 is a LINER-type galaxy. NGC 3300 is part of a galaxy group known as NGC 3306 Group or LGG 207. It has at least 7 confirmed members, which are NGC 3306, NGC 3300, UGC 5695, UGC 5739, UGC 5760, UGC 5758 and UGC 5781.
