NGC 6943 is a large barred spiral galaxy in the constellation of Pavo. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 3,049±6 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 146.7 ± 10.3 Mly (44.97 ± 3.15 Mpc). However, 17 non-redshift measurements give a much closer mean distance of 113.08 ± 2.32 Mly (34.671 ± 0.712 Mpc). It was discovered by British astronomer John Herschel on 27 June 1835. NGC 6943 has a possible active galactic nucleus, i.e. it has a compact region at the center of a galaxy that emits a significant amount of energy across the electromagnetic spectrum, with characteristics indicating that this luminosity is not produced by the stars.

SN 1998ew (Type II, mag. 14) was discovered by Roberto Antezana on 23 November 1998. SN 2005av (Type IIn, mag. 15.4) was discovered by Berto Monard on 24 March 2005.
