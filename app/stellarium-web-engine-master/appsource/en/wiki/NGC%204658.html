NGC 4658 is a spiral galaxy located in the constellation of Virgo. It was discovered by William Herschel on March 25, 1786. NGC 4658 is part of a galaxy group known as NGC 4658 Group or LGG 304, it has at least 6 confirmed members, which are NGC 4682, NGC 4680, NGC 4658, MCG 2-33-20, MCG 1-33-32 and MCG 2-33-10.
