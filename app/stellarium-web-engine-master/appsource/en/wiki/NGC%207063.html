NGC 7063 is an open cluster in Cygnus. It was discovered on August 19, 1828, by John Herschel. It is located near to the star 69 Cygni. The cluster was first studied in 1931 by Per Collinder, who believed the cluster to have only 10 to 12 stars. The distance was determined in 1961. In 2021, it was discovered that the cluster contained 75 stars.
