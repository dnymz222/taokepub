NGC 7735 is an elliptical galaxy in the constellation of Pegasus. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 9249 ± 36 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 136.41 ± 9.57 Mpc (~445 million light-years). It was discovered by British astronomer John Herschel on 5 September 1828. One supernova has been observed in NGC 7735: SN 2024we (Type Ia, mag 17) was discovered by ASAS-SN on 11 January 2024.
