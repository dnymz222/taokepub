NGC 192 is a spiral galaxy in the constellation Cetus. It was discovered on December 28, 1790, by William Herschel. NGC 192 is part of a galaxy group known as LGG 10, together with 4 core members: NGC 173, NGC 196, NGC 201, NGC 237 and 1 possible member: NGC 197. It is also considered part of HCG 7 along with NGC 201, NGC 196, and NGC 197.
