Omega1 Cygni is a solitary star in the northern constellation of Cygnus. Its name is a Bayer designation that is Latinized from ω1 Cygni and abbreviated Omega1 Cyg or ω1 Cyg. This star is visible to the naked eye with an apparent visual magnitude of 4.94. Based upon an annual parallax shift of 2.55 mas, it is estimated to lie at a distance of approximately 1,280 light years from the Sun. Relative to its neighbors, this star has a peculiar velocity of 25.7±2.2 km/s.

This is a somewhat evolved B-type subgiant star with a stellar classification of B2.5 IV. Telting and colleagues report it as a Beta Cephei variable with a high degree of confidence as they found regular pulsations in its spectrum in a high-resolution spectroscopy study published in 2006. Its brightness varies irregularly by 0.034 magnitude every 1.137 days.
