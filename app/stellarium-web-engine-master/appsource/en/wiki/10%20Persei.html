10 Persei is a blue supergiant star in the constellation Perseus. Its apparent magnitude is 6.26 although it is slightly variable. 10 Persei is located around 3,333 parsecs (10,870 ly) distant in the Perseus OB1 stellar association. It lies close to the Double Cluster and is considered a cluster member.

In 1999, 10 Persei was given the variable star designation V554 Persei, after being identified as varying in Hipparcos photometry. Its brightness varies by less than a tenth of a magnitude with no clear period.
