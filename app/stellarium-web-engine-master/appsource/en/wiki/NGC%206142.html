NGC 6142 is a large spiral galaxy in the constellation of Corona Borealis. Its velocity with respect to the cosmic microwave background is 10,180±4 km/s, which corresponds to a Hubble distance of 489.7 ± 34.3 Mly (150.15 ± 10.51 Mpc). It was discovered by German-British astronomer William Herschel on 30 May 1791.

Galaxy cluster

NGC 6142 is a member of the galaxy cluster known as Abell 2199.

Supernova

One supernova has been observed in NGC 6142:

SN 2006R (Type Ia, mag. 17.5) was discovered by Tim Puckett and Alex Langoussis on 26 January 2006.
